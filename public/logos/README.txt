Official university logos (optional, recommended)
-------------------------------------------------
Put a high-resolution PNG (at least 256x256, transparent or white background) named after the university's domain, e.g.

  kluniversity.in.png      (KL University, Vijayawada)
  klh.edu.in.png           (KL University, Hyderabad)
  gitam.edu.png

The app tries logos/<domain>.png first and falls back to the favicon services, then to a text monogram.
Only use logos you are permitted to use. The app never invents or guesses branding.
Domains used by the app are listed in UNI_DOMAIN inside index.html.
